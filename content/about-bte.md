# About BTE

BTE, short for Believers Tech Expo, is a technology community for students on Nigerian campuses. Campus chapters are hosted by partner fellowships and run by student leads.

## What BTE does

BTE is a community that host build sessions every term. A build session is sixty minutes, one practical skill, always hands on. Participants build real projects, ship real work and learn how software teams collaborate.

## How it is run

Chapters are run by pairs of student leads, a campus lead and an assistant lead. They organise meetings, keep the chapter active and hand the chapter over when they graduate.

## Where BTE is headed

The community runs a yearly expo where chapters present what they built across the term. The platform that runs applications, meetings and reviews is being built in the open, and the people who learned the contribution flow in build sessions are the ones building it.
