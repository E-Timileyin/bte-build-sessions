# BTE campus chapters

Chapters are the local homes of BTE on each campus. Each chapter runs its own build sessions and reports back to the community.

## Active chapters

- University of Lagos (UNILAG), Lagos. Founded 2025. Next build session: 16 May 2026.
- Obafemi Awolowo University (OAU), Ile-Ife. Founded 2025. Next build session: 15 May 2026.
- University of Benin (UNIBEN), Benin City. Founded 2026. Next build session: 22 May 2036.
- Obafemi Awolowo University (OAU), Ile-Ife. Founded 2025. Next build session: 15 May 2026.

## How a chapter starts

A pair of students, a campus lead and an assistant lead, find a host fellowship on their campus and run it reliably for a term. Then they apply, clear the approval gates, and the chapter launches.
